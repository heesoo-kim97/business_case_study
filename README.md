# Business Case Study

A collection of real-world and hypothetical business cases exploring problems at the intersection of **Business, Data, Operations, and Technology**.

This repository documents how I approach business problems from an analytical and operational perspective—from understanding the problem and identifying key stakeholders to analyzing data, developing insights, and recommending potential solutions.

## Purpose

The goal of this repository is to develop and demonstrate practical skills in:

* Business & operational analysis
* Data analysis and visualization
* Supply chain & logistics
* Process improvement
* Technology & system analysis
* KPI development
* Root-cause analysis
* Decision-making using data
* Translating business problems into technical requirements

## Case Study Framework

Each business case follows a structured approach:

1. **Business Problem** — What problem is the organization facing?
2. **Business Context** — How does the business/process work?
3. **Stakeholders** — Who is affected by the problem?
4. **Key Questions** — What questions need to be answered?
5. **Data** — What data would be needed?
6. **Analysis** — What does the data reveal?
7. **Root Cause** — Why is the problem occurring?
8. **Solution Options** — What could the organization do?
9. **Technology** — What systems or tools could support the solution?
10. **Recommendation** — What approach could address the problem?
11. **KPIs** — How would success be measured?
12. **Business Impact** — What could improve if the solution succeeds?

Cases will primarily focus on:

## Long-Term Goal

Build a portfolio demonstrating the ability to connect **business problems → data → operations → technology → measurable outcomes**.

Rather than focusing only on producing an analysis, each case asks:

> **What is the business trying to accomplish, what is preventing it from doing so, and how can data and technology help?**
